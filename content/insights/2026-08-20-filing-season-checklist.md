Every year, the same thing slows filers down: hunting for documents at the last minute. A little preparation makes the whole return quick and painless. Here is what to have ready before you start.

## Gather these first

- **CNIC** (front and back) and your IRIS login, if you already have one
- **Salary certificate** from your employer, showing tax deducted under section 149
- **Tax deduction certificates** from your bank, mobile operator, and on your vehicle and utility bills
- **Bank statements** for the full tax year
- Details of any **other income** you had: rent, business, or capital gains

## Why the wealth statement matters

Your declared assets and liabilities should move in line with your income. If your net worth jumps without the income to explain it, that gap gets flagged. Keeping simple records of what you bought, sold, and saved during the year makes this part straightforward.

## Do not wait for the deadline

Filing early avoids the last-week rush, gives time to fix anything missing, and keeps you on the active taxpayer list. If you are unsure whether you even need to file, or which income applies to you, it is worth a quick conversation before you start.
