If you sell goods or provide services, one question comes up early: do you need to register for sales tax? Here is a plain-language view of who it applies to and what the process looks like.

## Federal vs provincial

Sales tax in Pakistan is split. Tax on **goods** is federal, administered by FBR through a Sales Tax Registration Number (STRN). Tax on **services** is provincial, handled by the authority in your province &mdash; PRA in Punjab, SRB in Sindh, KPRA and BRA elsewhere. Which one applies depends on what you sell and where you operate.

## Who typically registers

- Manufacturers and importers of taxable goods
- Wholesalers, distributors and many retailers above a threshold
- Businesses supplying taxable services in a province that taxes them
- Anyone required to register to work with registered buyers or on tenders

## What the process involves

Registration is document-heavy: proof of business premises, bank details, utility connections, and, for manufacturers, photographs of the premises and machinery. It usually includes **biometric verification** at a NADRA e-Sahulat centre, and manufacturers can expect a **post-verification visit** of the premises.
