How we work

The principles behind every return we file and every service we run.

  • Filed the lawful way. Returns go through an authorized FBR e-intermediary acting as your appointed representative — we never ask for or store the IRIS password that belongs to you and FBR alone.
  • Correct, and defensible. A versioned rules engine computes every figure, sixty-one automated checks review it before filing, and a chartered accountant signs it off on our premium service.
  • Your data stays yours. Encrypted in transit and at rest, scoped per client, accessed only under role-based permission with the reason logged. We never sell data or market off your return.
  • Clear from the start. Every service lists the exact documents it needs and confirms the fee before you pay — then follows a tracked reference all the way to completion. In English or اردو.
