Privacy Policy

How Big1 Consultants (Private) Limited collects, uses and protects your information when you use FilePak, write to us on WhatsApp, or engage us for tax, corporate or intellectual-property services.

Effective 8 October 2026 · Big1 Consultants (Private) Limited, 224 A Iqbal Avenue, Phase II, Lahore 54770, Pakistan · info@big1.com.pk · +92 339 9999611

1. Who we are and what this covers

"We", "us" and "Big1" mean Big1 Consultants (Private) Limited, a company incorporated in Pakistan. This policy covers:

  • FilePak, our online tax-filing and services platform at app.big1.com.pk;
  • our WhatsApp business number (+92 339 9999611) and the automated assistant and staff who answer on it;
  • the WhatsApp messaging software we operate, which we also make available to other professional firms to run their own WhatsApp business numbers; and
  • the professional services we provide: income-tax and sales-tax filing, FBR, SECP and IPO registrations, intellectual-property work and related advice.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, mobile and WhatsApp number, email address and, where relevant, your business name and address.
  • Identity and tax information needed for the service you ask for: CNIC, NTN, registration numbers, income and expense details, bank details for refunds, and the documents you send us (salary certificates, bank statements, invoices, photos of documents).
  • WhatsApp messages: the messages, voice notes and files you exchange with our number, and the time they were sent, delivered and read.
  • FBR portal credentials: only when you ask us to file on your behalf. They are encrypted and used solely to prepare and submit your return.
  • Technical information: device and browser type, IP address, pages visited and actions taken, used to keep the service secure and working.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. Our services are for adults and businesses.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service you asked for: answer your questions, prepare and file returns and applications, register you with FBR, SECP, IPO or other authorities on your instruction, and deliver the results.
  • To communicate with you about your matter, including on WhatsApp. We send WhatsApp template messages (for example, a request for a document or a note that your work is ready) only to people who have contacted us or engaged us, and you can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To invoice and keep accounts.
  • To meet legal and professional obligations, including record-keeping required by Pakistani tax and corporate law.
  • To run and improve the service: our WhatsApp assistant answers routine questions automatically and may draft replies for our staff. For this, message text may be processed by Google Cloud's AI service (Vertex AI) within our own Google Cloud project. It is not used to train public models.

4. WhatsApp

Our WhatsApp number runs on the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta. Messages travel through WhatsApp under WhatsApp's business terms; we hold our own copy of the conversation and of any file you send us so that your matter can be handled and your documents are not lost. We do not sell your number or messages, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your consent.

5. Who we share it with

  • Authorities: FBR, SECP, IPO, provincial revenue authorities and banks, only to the extent needed to carry out your instruction.
  • Service providers who host or process data for us: Google Cloud (our systems and files are hosted in the Mumbai region), Meta (WhatsApp), and our email and payment providers. They act on our instructions and under contracts that protect your data.
  • Firms that use our WhatsApp software: if you write to another firm's number that runs on our software, that firm is responsible for your data and this policy applies to our role as their technology provider only.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect our rights and those of our clients.

We never sell personal information.

6. How long we keep it

We keep client records for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal record-keeping requirements, which for tax and corporate matters in Pakistan is generally six years after the engagement ends. WhatsApp conversations and files are kept on the client's file for the same period. FBR credentials are deleted when you ask us to, or when the engagement ends. Technical logs are kept for a short period for security purposes.

7. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and sensitive values such as portal credentials are encrypted at rest. Access inside Big1 is limited to the staff working on your matter, through named accounts with role-based permissions, and every change to a client file is logged. Staff who prepare work do not see customers' phone numbers unless the matter is allocated to them.

8. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop contacting you on WhatsApp. Write to info@big1.com.pk or message our WhatsApp number. Deletion requests are handled as described on our data deletion page. Where the law requires us to keep a record, we will tell you what must be kept and for how long.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here with a new effective date. Material changes affecting how we use your information will be notified to active clients.
