Why is my refund lower than last year?
FilePak Intelligence Three drivers. Your salary rose 11.9%, which pushed Rs 420,000 into the next slab. Bank withholding fell because two term deposits matured in October. And your investment tax credit is Rs 41,000 lower — the pension contribution stopped in March. Sources Section 149 · Section 7B · Section 63 · Ruleset FA-2025
Can I still claim anything?
Yes — contributing Rs 300,000 to an approved pension fund before 30 June would restore roughly Rs 45,000 of credit under Section 63. I’ve modelled it in a draft scenario for your review. Suggestion only · requires your approval before it touches the return
