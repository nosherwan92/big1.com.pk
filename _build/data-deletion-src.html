How to delete your data

You can ask Big1 Consultants (Private) Limited to delete the information we hold about you from FilePak and from our WhatsApp records. This page explains how, what is deleted, and what the law requires us to keep.

1. How to ask

  1. Send an email to info@big1.com.pk with the subject "Data deletion request", from the email address or with the WhatsApp number we have on record; or
  2. send the message "Delete my data" to our WhatsApp number +92 339 9999611.

We will confirm your identity using the details on record, then act on the request.

2. What happens

  • Within 30 days we delete your FilePak account, your profile, saved FBR credentials, uploaded documents, WhatsApp conversation history and files held on your client file, and we stop all WhatsApp messages to you.
  • We send you a confirmation when it is done.
  • Information shared with authorities (FBR, SECP, IPO) as part of a filing you instructed cannot be withdrawn from them by us.

3. What we must keep

Pakistani tax and corporate law requires professional firms to keep working papers, invoices and filed returns for a period after the work is done, generally six years. Where that applies, we keep only the records the law requires, restrict access to them, and delete everything else. We will tell you in our confirmation what was kept and until when.

4. Firms that use our WhatsApp software

If you wrote to another firm whose WhatsApp number runs on our software, that firm holds your data and decides on deletion. Send your request to that firm; if you cannot reach them, write to us and we will pass it on and confirm the outcome.
