How to delete your data

You can ask Big1 Consultants (Private) Limited to delete the information we hold about you from FilePak and from our WhatsApp records. This page explains how, what is deleted, and what the law requires us to keep.

1How to ask

By email
Write to us

Send info@big1.com.pk an email with the subject “Data deletion request”, from the email address or with the WhatsApp number we have on record.

On WhatsApp
Send one message

Send “Delete my data” to our number +92 339 9999611. A member of staff will pick it up.

We confirm your identity using the details on record, then act on the request.

2What happens

  • Within 30 days we delete your FilePak account, your profile, saved FBR credentials, uploaded documents, WhatsApp conversation history and files held on your client file, and we stop all WhatsApp messages to you.
  • We send you a confirmation when it is done.
  • Information already filed with authorities (FBR, SECP, IPO) on your instruction cannot be withdrawn from them by us.

3What we must keep

Pakistani tax and corporate law requires professional firms to keep working papers, invoices and filed returns for a period after the work is done, generally six years. Where that applies, we keep only the records the law requires, restrict access to them, and delete everything else. Our confirmation tells you what was kept and until when.

4Firms that use our WhatsApp software

If you wrote to another firm whose WhatsApp number runs on our software, that firm holds your data and decides on deletion. Send your request to that firm; if you cannot reach them, write to us and we will pass it on and confirm the outcome.

Contact

Deletion requests and questions about them go to the company directly.
