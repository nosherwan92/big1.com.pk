FEATURE 03

Withholding, reconciled to the rupee.

Pakistan taxes you at source in a dozen places. Most returns simply under-claim. FilePak pulls what FBR holds against your CNIC, matches it to your own records, and shows you exactly where the two disagree — before you file, not after a notice.

  • Matched, unmatched and disputed — three buckets, no ambiguity
  • Claim what is actually yours — the average missed credit is not small
  • Every difference carries a reason, recorded in the audit trail
